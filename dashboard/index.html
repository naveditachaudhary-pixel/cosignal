Four ways in

You don't have to start from a blank page.

A sentence, a profile you wrote years ago, the site you're embarrassed by, or nothing at all - each of these is a real way to begin, and each ends at a finished first draft.

  1. 01 Describe it

    Say what you do. In two sentences.

    The words you'd use with a customer are enough - add your logo and a few photos if you have them. A finished first draft builds while you watch, and nothing it asks you is technical.

  2. 02 Start from your LinkedIn

    You already wrote it. Paste the profile.

    Spanli reads your profile or company page, confirms your details, and builds a site from the experience you have spent years describing - your photo included.

  3. 03 Modernize the site you have

    Hand it the address you're embarrassed by.

    Spanli reads what your current site says and rebuilds it as something current, keeping your words. Or paste a site whose feel you admire and it borrows the mood, not the content.

  4. 04 I feel lucky

    Answer nothing at all.

    For anyone who freezes at choices: Spanli picks the pages, the sections and the look itself. Every decision it made is listed on the way through, and you can change any of them.
